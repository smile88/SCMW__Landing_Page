The Fixed-Price Commercial Guarantee

Prepared for Managing Directors, Financial Controllers, and Operations Buyers.
The Commercial Policy: S. C. Milenwall operates exclusively on fixed-price engagement scopes. Every project fee is agreed in writing prior to work commencing. We do not bill by the hour, and we absorb all unforeseen technical complexities within the agreed scope.

How We Handle Scope Adjustments

If your team requests an entirely new feature outside the Statement of Work, we provide an explicit written fixed-price addendum before touching a line of code. You are never billed retroactively.
