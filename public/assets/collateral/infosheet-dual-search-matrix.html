The Dual-Engine Search Matrix

Traditional Google Ranking Factors vs Modern Generative AI LLM Extraction.

In 2026, search optimisation requires a dual-engine architecture. A business cannot rely solely on legacy Google keyword crawlers; it must also structure its entity knowledge graph to be cited by generative AI answer engines.

Search Dimension Traditional Google SEO (Legacy) AI Answer Engines (ChatGPT, AIO)
Discovery Mechanism Web spider indexing keywords and URL strings. Embedding models parsing entity relationships & semantic meaning.
Primary Data Layer HTML page title, H1-H3 headers, and meta description. Schema.org JSON-LD structured data and knowledge graphs.
Authority Signal Domain rating and quantity of external backlinks. Verified entity records (ABR, ASIC), author EEAT credentials, client citations.
Query Format Short keywords: "commercial electrician sydney". Natural conversational prompts with multiple constraints and requirements.
User Presentation 10 blue links with 2-line snippets. Synthesised bullet-point recommendation with 1–2 direct vendor citations.

The SCM Engineering Rule

We never build a website or SEO campaign for just one engine. By deploying strict Schema.org JSON-LD (ProfessionalService, OfferCatalog, Founder) alongside plain-language commercial copywriting, your Sydney business wins both the Google click and the ChatGPT recommendation.
