Sales Enablement Standard // SCM-INF-03

The SMB Sales Playbook Standard

Stage-Gated Qualification, Deal Hygiene, and 48-Hour Proposal SLAs.

Drawing directly from enterprise cybersecurity quota sales (Mandiant, Fortinet), this document outlines the 4-stage pipeline architecture that eliminates 9pm quoting bottlenecks and accelerates commercial deal velocity.

STAGE 01: DISCOVERY & QUALIFICATION (Within 24 Hours) Max 20 Minutes

Identify whether the prospect matches your Ideal Customer Profile (ICP). Filter out tyre-kickers with strict commercial qualification gates.

Exit Criteria: Confirmed commercial budget framework, verified decision-maker timeline, identified core pain point.
STAGE 02: 48-HOUR FIXED-PRICE PROPOSAL Strict 48h SLA

Deliver a modular, beautifully formatted proposal detailing exact deliverables, milestones, and guaranteed fixed pricing.

Exit Criteria: Proposal received and opened by client; verbal or written acknowledgment of pricing structure.
STAGE 03: COMMERCIAL ALIGNMENT & BATTLECARDS 3–5 Business Days

Navigate procurement objections using tested battlecards. Address stakeholder concerns (e.g. board sign-off, scheduling, scope addendums).

Exit Criteria: Scope confirmed in writing; contract sent for digital signature.
STAGE 04: CLOSED-WON & ONBOARDING KICKOFF Immediate Handover

50% commencement invoice settled. Single point of accountability initiates project delivery timeline.
